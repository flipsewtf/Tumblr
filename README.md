# Tumblr Themes

Here you will find my Tumblr themes, guides, scripts, plugins, and base code.

> **Note:** As of May 2026, I am beginning to reorganise themes into their own subfolders, including their guides and info.

#### Looking for my premium themes?

They're available on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/flipse) ❤️

## Folders

- `Themes/` – Tumblr themes ready to install on your blog.
- `BaseCode/` – Accessible Tumblr base code template to start your own theme.
- `Plugins/` – Plugins for Tumblr themes.
